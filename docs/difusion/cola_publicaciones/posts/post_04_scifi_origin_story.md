# Building a sci-fi universe from a marble workshop using a cracked mobile phone: The origin of Universo Proiectio & Sapiensia Clan

Hi everyone.

I wanted to share a story about resilience, worldbuilding, and how modern technology can empower independent creators who don't have fancy setups or big publisher backing.

A little over a year ago, *Universo Proiectio* didn't start in a high-end design studio. It was written line by line on a cracked smartphone screen in the middle of a dusty marble workshop where I work with stone.

Whenever physical energy was drained, my creative fire wasn't. I partnered with AI (Gemini, whom I nicknamed *The Nexus*) not to generate lazy text, but as a co-architect to challenge lore, check structural pacing, and help me build a sprawling universe featuring biological resistance fighters, cybernetic dilemmas, and deep philosophical conflicts.

Today, that vision has grown into **SAPIENSIA Clan**:
- A published trilogy (**Euthanasys**, **Los Textos del Poeta**, **VELA**).
- Web minigames written in lightweight Vanilla JS.
- A fully transparent manifesto on human-AI synergy where the human soul remains the sole director.

If you are an indie author or worldbuilder struggling with limitations, remember: **your vision doesn't depend on your hardware; it depends on your will and heart.**
