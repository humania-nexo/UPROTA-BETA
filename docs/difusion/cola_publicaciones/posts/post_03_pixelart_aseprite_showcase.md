# [OC] [CC] Sprite animations and CRT aesthetic for our indie transmedia universe (Aseprite + HTML5 Canvas)

Hey r/PixelArt!

Sharing some sprite work and animation sheets created for our independent transmedia project **Sapiensia Clan** / **Universo Proiectio**.

### 🎨 The Aesthetic & Palette:
- **Style:** 16-bit cyber-arcade mixed with dystopian retro-futuristic vibes.
- **Tools:** Aseprite for frame-by-frame character loops (Orion, Mite the quantum drone, and the Frequency Sentinel) + Vanilla JS Canvas for procedural lighting and CRT scanline emulation.
- **Palette:** High-contrast phosphor neon cyan (`#00E5FF`), amber gold (`#FFE066`), and magenta glitch (`#FF0055`) over deep cosmic obsidian (`#060C18`).

We also designed dynamic cover variants for our sci-fi trilogy that seamlessly metamorphose between an organic editorial look and a full-blown 8-bit retro arcade view:
